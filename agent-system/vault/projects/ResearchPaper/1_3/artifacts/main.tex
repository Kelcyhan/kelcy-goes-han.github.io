\begin{abstract}
We present \emph{reflective companions}, a design framing for conversational AI that prioritises
guided self-reflection over engagement metrics. Through a 13-paper systematic review and an
upcoming N=24 diary study, we contribute (a) a taxonomy of companion design moves, (b)
operationalised metrics for reflective depth, and (c) a reference architecture that decouples
prompting strategy from delivery channel. We argue this framing opens an underexplored design space
between productivity tools and entertainment-style AI assistants.
\end{abstract}

\keywords{conversational AI, reflection, mental health, design}
