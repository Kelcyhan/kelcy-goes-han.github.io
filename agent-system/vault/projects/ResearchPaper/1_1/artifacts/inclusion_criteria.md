# Inclusion Criteria (PRISMA-Lite)

## Include if

- HCI-adjacent venue: CHI, CSCW, DIS, IUI, TOCHI, UIST, IMWUT, JMIR Mental Health
- Published 2020-01-01 through 2026-06-01
- Peer-reviewed (no preprints, except seminal foundational papers — flag separately)
- Empirical (> 6 participants), OR a system contribution evaluated by ≥ 2 expert reviewers
- Studies a conversational system OR a reflective intervention (digital or hybrid)

## Exclude if

- Pure clinical-outcome RCT (no UX/HCI dimension)
- Patent or industry whitepaper
- Tutorial / panel / position paper
- Replication-only without theoretical contribution
- Pre-2020 unless on the foundational allowlist (see allowlist.md)

## Foundational allowlist

A small set of pre-2020 papers may be included if cited frequently in 2020-26 work AND
explicitly establish a method or concept used in the modern wave.
