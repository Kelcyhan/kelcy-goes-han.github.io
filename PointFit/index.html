PointFit

Case Study · Health-tech

A non-invasive sweat biosensor brand for athletes who refuse the needle — senior interaction + brand design for the public site and the companion app surfaces.

Health-tech · Shipped 2024 2023.10 — 2024.06 Brand · Site · App · Components

Why sweat

Biochemistry, off the clinic.

Lactate, cortisol, glucose — today these readings still live in hospitals. PointFit moves them onto a skin patch, continuous and non-invasive.

"Biosensors will be the most important revolution since heart-rate monitors." — Dr. Iñigo San Millán, UAE Cycling Team coach.

Design criteria

Calm. Clinical. Athletic.

Three tensions to balance: credibility for clinicians, aspiration for athletes, legibility for data. Every component had to read as medical-grade and sports-ready at once.

Dark slate canvas + mint-green diagnostic accent + restrained athletic photography became the through-line.

Product & brand

The site, the system, the type.

Hero with athlete + product, three-tile feature row, exercise→result→improve carousel, press grid for Forbes 30U30, ICT Awards gold, HKTDC Start-up Express.

Slate · Mint · Cream · Ink. Display sans for headlines, quiet sans for body, animation components shipped to production.

App surfaces

From site to wrist.

The marketing promise lands on a phone — sweat data dashboards and live training mode. Two screen recordings from the companion app, autoplay-looped.

Brand language extended cleanly from web hero into in-app cards, sparklines, and live indicators.

Site blueprint

The whole scroll.

Full marketing-page rhythm — hero, "How it works", press strip, footer CTA. Drag horizontally to inspect the full Figma export.

Conversion path tuned for athlete + clinician audiences sharing the same screen.

Reflection

What sweat taught me.

Medical-tech branding lives or dies on tone — too clinical loses athletes; too hyped loses doctors. The components I shipped are now PointFit's design-system baseline.
