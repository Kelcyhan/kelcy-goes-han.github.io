MetacogReef

Case Study

A service-design ecosystem helping adolescents navigate self-identity through metacognitive therapy — across digital, spatial, and social touchpoints.

Individual Project 2023.02 — 2023.06 Service · Interaction · Spatial · Social

Introduction & Research

Designing the space between thoughts.

Adolescents form their sense of self in the gap between what they feel, what they're told, and what they observe. Metacognitive therapy gives them a vocabulary for that gap.

Surveys, observational fieldwork, and expert interviews with adolescent therapists surfaced six recurring tensions that shaped every downstream decision.

Design Criteria

Six anchors. One reef.

Empathy. Metacognitive Therapy. Self-Identification. Independence. Attachment. Resilience.

A checklist I ran each design choice against — surfacing tradeoffs early and keeping every artefact in coherent emotional register.

Product & Brand

A pocket reef, dressed in its own palette.

Wireframes to high-fidelity flows in Figma, tested with adolescent users over three rounds. Brand: earthy ochre and sand against deep coral — warm enough for teenagers, restrained enough for clinicians.

Linen · Sand · Rust · Teal · Ink. Editorial serif paired with quiet sans.

Spatial

An inner landscape, made walkable.

The exhibition translates the app's metaphor into physical space — a curved maze with a central reef-sphere, lit so visitors notice their own shadow before the artwork.

Service Blueprint

The whole choreography.

Every layer of the system — user actions, frontstage staff, backstage logic, and supporting evidence — mapped end-to-end.

Reflection

What the reef taught me.

Service design is the practice of designing things people walk through, not just things they hold. MetacogReef pushed me to keep the same care across every modality.

Future work explores partnering with a Hong Kong NGO to pilot the daily-reef ritual in school counsellor sessions.
